MadeMem policies

Cookie Policy

You can read the site while optional tags stay off.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Essential browser storage

We store a cookie preference in localStorage so this browser remembers your choice. The memorial demonstration can store a text draft locally if you choose Save. These functions do not require an advertising tag. Local storage may be unavailable in some browser modes.

Analytics and marketing

Optional slots exist for Meta Pixel, Google tag, and TikTok Pixel. Their IDs are currently empty, so no tag requests load. If an ID is later configured, analytics requires analytics consent and advertising requires marketing consent. Decline keeps both categories off.

What optional events mean

The prepared event names are page_view, preview_start, whatsapp_click, and search. WhatsApp events refer to opening the number panel or copying the number. No optional event is sent without a configured ID and the matching consent. Inquiry messages, email addresses, memorial drafts, and photos are not included in event payloads.

Your choice

Choose Decline optional, Analytics only, or Accept optional. Reopen Cookie settings in the footer to change the choice. If you withdraw consent after a configured tag has loaded, the site reloads to stop it. Previously set third-party cookies may also be managed through your browser settings.

Global Privacy Control

When the browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat it as an opt-out of sale or sharing and keep advertising off. You can still choose analytics. The Do Not Sell or Share page also offers a browser-level opt-out and an email path.

Stored preference

The preference and timestamp remain in this browser until you change or clear them. Clearing site data removes the saved choice and any locally saved memorial draft. This is the policy we work to, and we may update it.
