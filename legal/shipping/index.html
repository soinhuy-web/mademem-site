Shipping Policy

Made in China. Ships from China. Here is what each part of the timeline means.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Making, ship-out, and transit

Ship-out is when the parcel leaves the workshop. Transit is its journey after that. The clock starts at proof approval. Each package page states its own window.

The usual ship-out window

Each package page states its ship-out window, counted from proof approval. Transit time is confirmed at order. We do not yet have measured transit times. Tracking is provided when the parcel ships. We confirm the transit information available at order.

Pieces with longer windows

Legacy typically leaves the workshop about 15–20 days after proof approval. Suzhou embroidery and plush making times are to be confirmed at preview. Dafen oil painting as a stand-alone or upgrade has its making time confirmed in the preview, often longer than a standard plaque.

US shipping and duties

US shipping included; duties confirmed at order. Made in China. Ships from China. We do not publish an unconfirmed dispatch city.

If timing changes

We must have a basis for a time we state. If we will miss that time, we will notify you and offer the choice to wait or cancel for a refund. We will discuss revised timing with you instead of silently extending it.

Sending a gift

We can ship to the purchaser or a gift recipient at a US address. Confirm the address before approval. A gift has a blank packing slip with no price, and a gift box where its package lists one. Tell us about a birthday or holiday before deciding. International ship-out is not a US overnight gift, and holiday cut-off dates are to be confirmed.

Questions before shipping

Contact info@teruu.com with your inquiry or order details. Clinic postage and commercial terms are to be confirmed separately. This is the shipping policy we work to, and we may update it.
