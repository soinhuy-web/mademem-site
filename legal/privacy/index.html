Privacy Policy

How MadeMem uses the information you choose to share.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Who operates this site

MadeMem is operated by Jingyun (Sichuan) Trading Co., Ltd. Contact info@teruu.com to ask about your information, request a correction, or ask for deletion.

What you share

We receive the email address, name, message, and photographs you send through your own email service. If an order proceeds, we need the recipient and shipping details to make and send it. There is no customer-account backend on this static site.

Why we use it

We use the information to answer you, prepare proofs, arrange an approved order, and provide after-sales help. Production is in China. Workshop staff may need a photograph and the relevant personalization details to make your piece.

Photographs deserve care

Photographs can be sensitive. We do not use your photos in advertising without separate permission. Send only photographs you have the right to use, and avoid unnecessary information about other people.

Browser storage and optional tags

The browser can store your cookie choice and an on-device memorial text draft. Your draft is not uploaded. A selected demo photo stays in session memory. Optional analytics and marketing tags require consent and a configured ID. IDs are currently empty, so those tags are inactive. Browser consent preferences are stored locally, with a timestamp.

Your choices

You can decline optional tags, reopen Cookie settings, or use the Do Not Sell or Share page. We honor Global Privacy Control as an advertising opt-out. You may contact us about access, correction, or deletion. We will explain any information we need to retain for an order or applicable obligations.

Retention and security

The retention period for proofs, production files, and hosted memorial pages is to be confirmed. Ask us before sending information if this affects your decision. The published site uses HTTPS. Your email provider handles the email you choose to send, under its own terms.

Adults and policy updates

The site is for adults and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact us. This is the policy we work to, and we may update it. Requests about privacy can be sent to the contact email shown below.
