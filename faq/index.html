Answers, without a rush

Questions you may have.

Start with the short answers, or explore the details below.

What do pet packages cost?

Comfort is $49, Warmth $79, Keepsake $129, Essential $129, Classic $249, and Legacy $499. Each includes the pieces listed on its page. Comfort is an acrylic suncatcher, sympathy card, and gift box.

Are US shipping and duties included?

The price includes shipping to a US address and US import duties. You should not receive a separate duty bill on delivery.

When does it ship?

Generally ships within 7–15 days after you approve the proof. Transit time is confirmed at order. Legacy typically takes about 15–20 days before ship-out. Plush typically takes 2–3 weeks, to be confirmed. Suzhou embroidery typically takes 6–10 weeks, confirmed in the preview.

What does the 24-hour proof promise mean?

Send one photo. We send 3 design proofs within 24 hours, counted in US business days, US Eastern Time. You choose 1 proof. 2 rounds of revision are included. You pay only after you approve the proof. We do not start production before approval. Friday evening, Saturday, Sunday, and US federal holidays do not start a 24-hour weekday clock. We count US business days in US Eastern Time.

When do I pay?

Only after you approve the proof. We then send payment instructions. The payment channel is being set up, and the exact steps will be confirmed in email.

Do I need to mail ashes?

Standard products do not require mailing ashes. You fill urns and vials yourself at home. Special custom plans — contact us.

Where are the pieces made?

Made in China. Ships from China. We bring pieces together for quality checks and packing.

What if it arrives damaged?

Send us photos. For quality or transit damage, we reship. You do not need to prepay a return to China.

Basics

What is included in a package?

The You get list on each package page is the complete starting point. Comfort has an acrylic suncatcher, card, and gift box. Warmth includes a plaque, card, gift box, and memorial page. Other sets list their own pieces.

What materials can I choose?

Wood, ceramic, metal, textile and embroidery, acrylic, and crystal are available in the collection or by custom-quote inquiry. Material choices and details are confirmed in the preview.

Where do I find living-pet gifts?

Use the Living pets page for oil portraits, plush, and birthday portraits. It has its own collection and inquiry path.

Timing

When will it arrive?

Transit time is confirmed at order. A ship-out window describes when the parcel leaves the workshop. We do not yet have measured transit times.

Which pieces take longer?

Legacy typically leaves the workshop about 15–20 days after proof approval. Suzhou embroidery typically takes 6–10 weeks, confirmed in the preview. Plush typically takes 2–3 weeks, to be confirmed. Stand-alone Dafen oil painting timing is confirmed in the preview.

What happens if the stated ship-out time changes?

We will notify you and offer a choice to wait or cancel for a refund if we will miss the stated time.

Photos

What if I do not have a good photo?

Send the clearest photo you have and tell us what matters in it. We can discuss a portrait, a composite, or restoration. Composite and restored images are identified in the proof.

Can I use an old or printed photograph?

You can send a clear scan or a straight, well-lit photograph of the print. Keep the original safe and tell us about any damage you want discussed.

Can I mail hair?

You fill the standard hair vial at home. For special custom plans, contact us. We will discuss what is possible individually.

Preview & payment

How many design changes are included?

We send 3 proofs. You choose 1, with 2 revision rounds included. You approve the final proof before production starts.

Does the form upload my photo?

The inquiry form opens your own email app. Attach your photo there and send the message yourself. There is no server-side photo upload.

Is there an online checkout?

Payment instructions are sent after proof approval. The payment channel is being set up. The exact steps will be confirmed in email.

Size

How do I choose an urn size?

Allow about 1 cubic inch per 1 pound of healthy body weight as a planning guideline. Confirm with the crematory. We confirm the size in writing in your preview. If that confirmed size is wrong, we remake it at no charge. Full-size human urns hold 200 cubic inches or more.

What if the written preview size is wrong?

If the size we confirm in writing in your preview is wrong, we remake it at no charge.

Are the dimensions final?

Typical size — confirmed in your preview. Sizes can be customized on request. Finished dimensions, wood species, and any textile fiber content are confirmed in your preview or on the label.

Shipping

Can you send a gift to a friend?

Yes. Tell us the recipient address. Gifts have a blank packing slip with no price, and a gift box where the package includes one.

Will I receive tracking?

We provide tracking when the parcel ships. Transit time is confirmed at order.

Can I order for a birthday or holiday?

Tell us the date before deciding. International ship-out is not a US overnight gift. Holiday cut-off dates are to be confirmed.

After-sales

Can I return a personalized piece if I change my mind?

Personalized goods have no change-of-mind returns. Please use the proof stage to check the design and details.

What if I damage the piece after arrival?

Damage you cause after arrival is your responsibility. Contact us to discuss whether a repair or replacement can be arranged.

What if you send the wrong item?

We are responsible and arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate. Refund decisions depend on who is at fault.

Memorial pages

How long is a memorial page kept?

The hosting and retention period is to be confirmed. We will confirm the terms before you proceed.

Is my page public?

Hosted memorial pages are private by default. The on-device demonstration stays in your browser and uploads nothing.

Does every package have a page?

Only packages that list a memorial page include one. Comfort does not include a memorial page. Check the You get list on your chosen set.
