What to say when a friend loses a pet

A few plain words can acknowledge a loss without asking your friend to explain it.

By MadeMem · Last updated November 11, 2026

Begin with the pet’s name

“I’m so sorry about Sam. I know how much he meant to you.” A direct acknowledgment can be enough. You do not need to find a sentence that changes how your friend feels.

If you knew the pet, share a small memory. Mention the greeting at the door, a favorite resting place, or an ordinary habit. Keep the focus on their companion.

Let your friend set the pace

Some people want to talk. Some want company without a conversation. You can ask, “Would you like to tell me about her, or would you prefer a quiet visit?” Accept either answer.

Avoid asking for details of the death unless your friend chooses to share them. A short message that does not require a reply can be easier to receive during a difficult week.

Offer something specific

“I can bring dinner on Thursday” gives your friend a simple choice. Offer something you can actually do. A walk, an errand, or help with an ordinary task may be welcome.

Check before moving the pet’s belongings or arranging a memorial object. Decisions about bowls, beds, ashes, and photographs are personal. Leave room for the family to choose.

Remember beyond the first message

A later check-in can acknowledge that the loss is still part of daily life. You might say, “I was thinking about Sam today. No need to reply.”

If you want to send a gift, a card can travel with it. Choose an object only when you know it will suit the recipient, and let them decide where it belongs.

When more support is wanted

Friends can offer company. If your friend wants grief support, an established pet-loss resource may help them find a conversation that fits. This article is not medical or counseling advice.

You can share a resource gently and leave the choice with them.

The Association for Pet Loss and Bereavement offers pet-loss support resources. Visit APLB

When you feel ready, you can ask a question or explore a piece at your own pace.
